# Calibration Examples

Eight real postings Chad graded during skill construction. When a new posting resembles one of these patterns, reason the same way. Each entry: verdict, the deciding factors, and the transferable lesson.

## 1. Ecosystems — CPTO — APPLY (fit ≈ 92)

Remote, reports to CEO, PE-backed (Gauge), profitable with strong retention, evolving into an AI-native agentic platform. **Standardized internally on Claude Code.** Domain = B2B GTM SaaS (Chad's home turf).
- Caveat: scope stretch **up** — CPTO owns Product AND Engineering. Close it with Salesforce dev-platform/CI-CD ownership, agentic prototyping framework, hands-on building.
- **Lesson:** when a posting names Chad's exact tools/identity (Claude Code, AI-first operating model), flag it as a rare high-signal match and lead the diff + why-me with the builder layer. Scope-up is a positive caveat, not a gap.

## 2. Amplify — EVP, Platform & AI Transformation — APPLY (fit ≈ 78)

Remote. K-12 EdTech, recently funded, 20M+ students. Textbook archetype (b): "transitioning from a collection of products into a cohesive, AI-integrated platform company." Coach-player mandate, wants technical depth that earns engineering respect.
- Caveat: **vertical gap** (EdTech — rostering, classroom, student privacy). Tailorable; address head-on in why-me.
- Title nuance: EVP carved out **alongside** an existing CPO, owns platform P&L → legitimate peer-exec mandate, **NOT** the VP-under-VP red flag.
- **Lesson:** distinguish peer C-level carve-out from buried-VP. Vertical gaps are tailorable when the discipline (enterprise platform product) is Chad's.

## 3. Ivo — Head of Product — APPLY_PENDING_DILIGENCE (fit ≈ 80)

SF-based legal-tech contract intelligence. ARR 6x in nine months, valuation ~5x in the same window, $55M Series B, Fortune 500 adoption up 250%, agentic pipeline (400+ model calls per review). Archetype (a), very high upside.
- Open question (gates the verdict): **is "Head of Product" the top product seat at this ~60-person company, or a layer down under a founder-CEO?** No CPO listed → plausibly top seat, but confirm.
- Lighter vertical gap (legal — enterprise buyer motion is close to Chad's world). Crowded-category risk worth raising in interviews, not disqualifying.
- **Lesson:** thin recruiter blurbs get researched (web search) before scoring. Ambiguous titles become open questions, not guesses. Vertical-gap severity ladder: legal < EdTech.

## 4. Brex — Group Product Manager — SOFT_SKIP (fit ≈ 45)

AI-native fintech, strong platform, explicitly wants aggressive AI-tool usage and architecture fluency (direct hit on Chad's builder identity). But: GPM = level below the bar; base $280–350K only grazes the floor at the very top; hybrid 3 days/week with SF as one option.
- **Lesson:** the **soft skip**. One-or-two near-miss gates, work itself attractive. Gets a *real* escape hatch: "if it's a mistitled VP-track role or total comp lands meaningfully above $350K with remote flex, worth a 20-min recruiter call. Otherwise pass."

## 5. Robinhood — Staff PM, Cortex — HARD_SKIP (fit ≈ 25)

Frontier consumer AI (agentic, evals at scale) — the AI substance is gorgeous. But: Staff IC seat, base tops at $225K (roughly half the floor), hybrid Menlo Park/NYC.
- **Lesson:** a shiny AI mandate never overrides blown hard gates. Say what's great, then skip cleanly. Hard skip escape hatch is honest: level+comp+location are all wrong; nothing tailorable.

## 6. Google/YouTube — PM, Trust & Safety — HARD_SKIP (fit ≈ 12)

Prestigious logo, IC PM seat, base $240–334K under floor, and — decisively — the mandate is **defensive integrity work** (anti-abuse, blocking engagement inflation) on a mature flat mega-platform. Off-thesis: no AI-growth mandate, no upside.
- **Lesson:** **prestige is not a criterion.** Grade the seat and the mandate, not the brand. A role can be at a famous AI-adjacent company and still be off-thesis. Three independent misses = pass without a call.

## 7. CoreWeave — VP, Product AI/ML — HARD_SKIP, discipline wall (fit ≈ 30)

Title clears the bar (VP, owns product strategy + eng execution). Maximally AI-native (the AI cloud), public, huge momentum. But requirements are Slurm/Kubernetes/InfiniBand, multi-thousand-GPU clusters, RL/RLHF infra, frontier-research background — plus base $233–341K under floor.
- **Lesson:** the **discipline wall**. Right title + right thesis can still be the wrong discipline. GPU/HPC infrastructure product ≠ application-layer product. Not tailorable — Chad has no credible claim and the resume cannot manufacture one. Escape hatch: only if they'd take an app-layer exec to complement infra ICs; requirements read hard-infra, so probably not.

## 8. Anthropic — PM, Developer Productivity — HARD_SKIP on criteria; ROCKET_SHIP_EXCEPTION available (fit ≈ 35 / 55 with override)

Comp $385–595K — the only posting whose floor clears Chad's floor. Real thesis alignment (AI reshaping how software gets built = Chad's lived thesis). But: IC PM seat, and the discipline is build systems / CI-CD / monorepos / accelerator toolchains — dev-infra platform product, the far side of the wall. Chad *uses* Claude Code daily; he has not *built the build system*.
- **Lesson 1:** the **keyword-overlap trap** — "developer productivity + AI + Claude" feels like Chad by keywords and is still a discipline wall. Check the actual required discipline, not the vocabulary.
- **Lesson 2:** the **rocket-ship override in action.** Anthropic is on the named list → level gate is waived (Chad will take IC to get in). But the override does not waive the discipline wall → the output is: honest scorecard ("on your criteria: skip — discipline wall"), then the exception block redirecting to app-layer product seats *at Anthropic* that match his discipline, plus — if he still wants this exact seat — the honest gap he'd have to sell past and a gut-check that he's deliberately overriding his own bar.

## Cross-cutting rules distilled

1. Grade the seat, not the brand (YouTube) and not the keywords (Anthropic).
2. Vertical gap ≠ discipline wall (Amplify/Ivo vs CoreWeave/Anthropic). Only the first is tailorable.
3. Peer-exec carve-out ≠ buried VP (Amplify).
4. Ambiguity → open question + PENDING_DILIGENCE, never a guess (Ivo).
5. Skip severity is a spectrum: calibrate the escape hatch (Brex vs Robinhood/YouTube).
6. High IC comp does not convert an IC seat into an exec seat (Anthropic).
7. Rocket-ship waives level only, targets the company, and always shows the honest scorecard first (Anthropic).
8. Great AI + blown gates = skip that still praises what's great (Robinhood, Brex).
