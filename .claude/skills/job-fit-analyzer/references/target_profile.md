Before that VP of Product at Pendo (Adopt 0→$50M ARR) and Salesforce (Hyperforce, developer platform, orgs of 1,000+). In an active search for his next seat.

## Hard gates (all must pass unless rocket-ship override)

1. **Level:** VP Product, CPO, CPTO, EVP Product, or peer C-level product carve-out. The top product seat or a legitimate exec peer to it. FAIL: PM, Senior PM, Staff PM, Principal PM, GPM, Director owning a single area under a product org. AMBIGUOUS (→ diligence question, verdict APPLY_PENDING_DILIGENCE if otherwise strong): "Head of Product" at a small company — could be the top seat, could be a layer down under a founder or CPO.
2. **Comp:** $350K+ floor (OTE/total for the seat). Signals: a base band with a ceiling under $350K priced as an IC/PM band = fail. A band that only reaches $350K at the very top = soft signal (grazing the floor), counts against but is arguable with equity. Exec packages judged on total.
3. **Location:** Remote = pass. SF/Bay Area = pass, including in-office or hybrid in the Bay Area. In-office/hybrid required anywhere outside the Bay Area = fail. A Bay Area office option attached to a role that fails other gates does not rescue it.
4. **Discipline:** Application/platform-layer B2B software product leadership (Chad's discipline). A role whose required discipline is dev-infra, build/CI systems, HPC/GPU/accelerator infrastructure, frontier-research infra, or hardware = **discipline wall = fail**, regardless of title, comp, or how AI-native it is.

## Fit thesis (both archetypes acceptable)

- **(a) AI-native and climbing:** AI is the core of the product, company has demonstrable growth (ARR multiples, valuation steps, Fortune 500 adoption, category-defining language backed by numbers).
- **(b) Established + needs AI:** proven company at an explicit AI-transformation inflection ("transitioning from a collection of products into an AI-integrated platform"), hiring this seat to lead it.

**Upside is required.** Chad optimizes for company trajectory: will it grow, or is it flat? Mature flat mega-platforms, defensive mandates (trust & safety, anti-abuse, integrity, pure maintenance), and roles with no equity/growth story are low-upside → probably skip, said plainly. Post-IPO is acceptable but note the equity upside differs from pre-IPO.

## Red flags (auto-flag in every analysis)

- VP of Product **reporting into** another VP/SVP of Product (not the top seat). Distinguish from a peer C-level carve-out alongside an existing CPO with its own P&L — that is legitimate, not a red flag.
- Product-marketing role mislabeled as product.
- Not remote AND not SF/Bay Area (hard gate 3, but call it out explicitly).
- Defensive/off-thesis mandate wearing an attractive logo.
- Keyword-overlap seduction: postings that name Chad's tools or identity words ("developer productivity", "AI agents", "Claude") but whose required discipline is on the far side of the wall.
